Privacy statement

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This website, s0tool.nl, is run by Huizebruin.nl. This statement explains which data we process, why, and what you can do about it. Questions? Email support@s0tool.nl.

What we measure, and why

Only if you click Accept in the cookie banner, we use Google Analytics to measure:

  • which pages are viewed;
  • where visitors come from (the website or search engine that sent you);
  • which firmware configuration is selected and installed on the installation page (selected, install started, install completed, install error), so we know which firmware is used the most and can improve it.

We use this to improve the website and the firmware, and for no other purpose. IP addresses are anonymised in Google Analytics.

Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). Without your consent nothing is measured.

Cookies and your choice

  • Before you choose, and if you click Decline: Google Analytics is not loaded, nothing is sent to Google and no analytics cookies are set. The website works exactly the same.
  • If you click Accept: Google Analytics is loaded and sets its cookies (names starting with _ga).
  • Your choice (accepted or declined, the date and time, and the version of the banner text) is stored only in your own browser (local storage, key s0tool-cookie-consent). We do not keep a copy on a server.
  • Change your mind at any time with Cookie settings in the footer of every page. If you decline afterwards, the Google Analytics cookies are removed. If the banner text changes, we ask you again.

Content from other parties

Some content on this website is loaded from other parties. When your browser loads it, that party receives technical data such as your IP address and browser information. This happens whether or not you accept analytics cookies:

  • img.shields.io: badges (release, license, stars).
  • storage.ko-fi.com and www.paypalobjects.com: support buttons.
  • my.home-assistant.io: Home Assistant buttons.
  • cdn.jsdelivr.net: icons. unpkg.com: the installer script (ESP Web Tools).
  • api.github.com: the number of the latest release.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website, so GitHub receives the requests for every page and file.

Links to shops (some are affiliate links) and to other websites take you away from this website. Their own privacy statements apply there. The firmware is installed through your browser (Web Serial); the installation itself sends nothing to us.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restriction or objection to the processing, and data portability. You can withdraw your consent at any time (see Cookie settings above); this does not affect what was done before. Email support@s0tool.nl. You also have the right to complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
